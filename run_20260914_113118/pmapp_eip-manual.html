A new account was created when you signed in with your new Google email, and pmapp_eip does not allow changing the email address directly from the interface. You have two options to resolve this.

This guide applies to everyone migrating from a short email address to the firstname.lastname format. Use your own email addresses in the options below.

Option 1 — Create a new account

Accept the new account created with your new email address (format: firstname.lastname@efficientip.com). Recreate your workspace, settings, and data on the new account. Recommended if you have little data to migrate.

Option 2 — Keep the old account

You can continue using your old account by logging in with your old short-format email address (the address you used before the migration) and your password (not via Google SSO). Recommended if you have important data on the original account.

If neither option suits you, contact pmapp_eip support directly — some apps allow email changes through a support request even if it's not exposed in the UI.

Verify and set up your Claude access

Access your AI assistant through your new migrated address. Follow this guide to sign in with your new credentials, verify your workspace, and recover your conversation history.

Estimated time: 5 min 5 steps Updated September 2026
0 / 5 done
1
Step 1 of 5
Sign out of your current Claude account

Before switching to your new account, make sure you are fully signed out of your current session.

  1. a
    Go to claude.ai in your browser.
  2. b
    Click on your profile picture or initials in the top-right corner.
  3. c
    Select Log out from the dropdown menu.
If you use the Claude desktop app, also sign out from there to avoid session conflicts.
2
Step 2 of 5
Sign in with your new migrated email address

Use your new corporate email address to authenticate and access your Claude workspace.

  1. a
    On the Claude login page, enter your new email address (e.g. firstname.lastname@newdomain.com).
  2. b
    Click Continue with email or sign in via SSO if your organization has it configured.
  3. c
    Check your new inbox for a verification link or one-time code and complete the authentication.
If your company uses SSO (Single Sign-On), use the "Continue with SSO" option and enter your company domain instead.
3
Step 3 of 5
Verify your workspace and plan access

After signing in, confirm that your account plan and workspace settings have been correctly migrated.

  1. a
    Check that the top of the page shows the correct organization name or team workspace.
  2. b
    Navigate to Settings → Account and confirm your new email address is displayed.
  3. c
    Under Settings → Plan, verify that you have the expected plan (Pro, Team, or Enterprise).
If you see a Free plan instead of your expected plan, contact your IT administrator — your seat may not have been transferred yet.
4
Step 4 of 5
Check your Projects and conversation history

Confirm that your previous work — Projects, saved conversations, and custom instructions — is accessible with your new account.

  1. a
    In the left sidebar, open Recents and verify that your past conversations appear.
  2. b
    Navigate to Projects and check that your existing projects and their documents are intact.
  3. c
    Open a recent conversation to confirm the content and history are readable.
Conversation history is tied to the account, not the browser. If history appears empty, try clearing the browser cache and signing in again.
5
Step 5 of 5
Update your bookmarks and connected integrations

To complete the migration, update any references or integrations that were linked to your old account.

  1. a
    Update your browser bookmark for claude.ai to reflect you are now logged in with the correct account.
  2. b
    If you use the Claude desktop app, sign in there as well with your new credentials.
  3. c
    If you have any API keys linked to the old account (via console.anthropic.com), generate new keys under the new account and update your services.
Old API keys from the previous account will stop working. Rotate them promptly to avoid service interruptions.

Migration complete!

Your Claude account is now set up with your new email address. You're all set to continue using Claude as usual.
